# Instructor imagery

`lockscreen.png` shows NOAA-20 water-vapor observations over the Pacific on 26 January 2021 at 14:46 UTC. NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio provides the animation, its color scale, and the original credits. [NASA visualization 4939](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4939/)

The terrain slide uses Daniel E. Coe’s elevation map of the Willamette River, published by the Oregon Department of Geology and Mineral Industries. The original poster includes the elevation color key. [Oregon lidar poster](https://pubs.oregon.gov/dogami/ll/p-poster-willamette.htm)
